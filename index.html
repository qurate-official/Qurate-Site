Selling to classmates shouldn't be a gamble.

Students have always sold things to each other. Textbooks, calculators, lab gear, furniture. But the tools were never built for them: Facebook groups, WhatsApp broadcasts and Instagram DMs.

We heard the same story in over 200 student interviews: people who got scammed, overpaid, or gave up searching after days. Most of those losses are preventable. They happen because the platforms weren't built with student safety in mind.

Qurate exists so that buying from a classmate feels as safe as buying from a store.
